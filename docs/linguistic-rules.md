# Amharic Number Linguistic & Grammar Rules

This document outlines the linguistic rules, orthography, and grammatical composition used by `amharic-number-words`.

---

## 1. Ethiopic Orthography and Vocabulary

### Digits (0 to 9)

| Digit | Fidel (ፊደል) | Transliteration | Notes |
|:-----:|:-----------|:----------------|:------|
| 0 | ዜሮ | zero | Standalone 0 |
| 1 | አንድ | and | Base unit |
| 2 | ሁለት | hulet | Base unit |
| 3 | ሦስት / ሶስት | sost | Traditional ሦስት, modern simplified ሶስት |
| 4 | አራት | arat | Base unit |
| 5 | አምስት | amist | Base unit |
| 6 | ስድስት | sidist | Base unit |
| 7 | ሰባት | sebat | Base unit |
| 8 | ስምንት | simint | Base unit |
| 9 | ዘጠኝ | zetegn | Base unit |

### Teens (10 to 19)

In Amharic, 10 is **አስር** (or archaic **ዐስር**). When compounding with 1–9, the final consonant shifts to 4th order 'ራ' (**አስራ**):

- 10: አስር
- 11: አስራ አንድ
- 12: አስራ ሁለት
- 13: አስራ ሦስት
- 14: አስራ አራት
- 15: አስራ አምስት
- 16: አስራ ስድስት
- 17: አስራ ሰባት
- 18: አስራ ስምንት
- 19: አስራ ዘጠኝ

### Tens (20 to 90)

- 20: ሃያ
- 30: ሰላሳ
- 40: አርባ
- 50: ሃምሳ (colloquial: አምሳ)
- 60: ስድሳ
- 70: ሰባ
- 80: ሰማንያ
- 90: ዘጠና

### Compound Numbers (21 to 99)

In English, compound numbers use hyphens (*twenty-five*). In French, conjunctions are used (*vingt-et-un*).
In Amharic:
- The tens and units are juxtaposed with a single space.
- **No conjunction** (*እና*) is used in Amharic numbers.
- Examples:
  - 25 = **ሃያ አምስት**
  - 48 = **አርባ ስምንት**
  - 99 = **ዘጠና ዘጠኝ**

## 2. Hundreds (100 to 999)

- Base word: **መቶ** (meto)
- **100**: In everyday speech, often called **መቶ**, but in formal writing, legal contracts, invoices, and cheques, **አንድ መቶ** is standard and required to prevent fraudulent alteration.
- **101**: **አንድ መቶ አንድ**
- **150**: **አንድ መቶ ሃምሳ**
- Multiples:
  - 200 = **ሁለት መቶ**
  - 500 = **አምስት መቶ**
  - 900 = **ዘጠኝ መቶ**

---

## 3. Scale Words (Thousands, Millions, Billions)

Amharic uses the short scale (groups of 1,000 / $10^{3n}$):

| Power | Amharic | Example | Wording |
|:------|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| $10^3$ | ሺህ (formal) / ሺ | 1,000 | አንድ ሺህ |
| $10^6$ | ሚሊዮን | 1,000,000 | አንድ ሚሊዮን |
| $10^9$ | ቢሊዮን | 1,000,000,000 | አንድ ቢሊዮን |
| $10^{12}$ | ትሪሊዮን | $10^{12}$ | አንድ ትሪሊዮን |
| $10^{15}$ | ኳድሪሊዮን | $10^{15}$ | አንድ ኳድሪሊዮን |
| $10^{18}$ | ኩይንቲሊዮን | $10^{18}$ | አንድ ኩይንቲሊዮን |

### Grammatical Grouping Rules

1. **No Connector Words**: In English, one writes *one thousand, five hundred and twenty*. In Amharic, no conjunctions appear between groups:
   - 1,520 = **አንድ ሺህ አምስት መቶ ሃያ**
2. **Leading "አንድ"**:
   - For thousands ($10^3$), 1,000 is **አንድ ሺህ**.
   - For millions and above ($10^6+$), 1 unit always requires **አንድ** (**አንድ ሚሊዮን**, **አንድ ቢሊዮን**).
3. **Compound Scales**:
   - 2,500,000 = **ሁለት ሚሊዮን አምስት መቶ ሺህ**

## 4. Preposition Attachment ("ከ")

In Amharic, **ከ** is a proclitic preposition meaning "and / with / from" that connects the major currency and minor currency units:

- When followed by Amharic words, **ከ** attaches directly:
  - **ብር ከሃምሳ ሳንቲም** (*Birr with fifty santim*)
  - **ብር ከአምስት ሳንቲም** (*Birr with five santim*)
- When followed by Latin Arabic numerals (e.g. in cheque fraction mode), a space is maintained:
  - **ብር ከ 50/100 ሳንቲም**
- When the major currency unit is zero (e.g. 0.75 ETB), **ከ** is omitted:
  - **ሰባ አምስት ሳንቲም** (*75 Santim*)
